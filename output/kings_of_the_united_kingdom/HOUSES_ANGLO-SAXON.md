# Anglo-Saxon Houses of the United Kingdom

These are the Anglo-Saxon dynasties that preceded William I (the Conqueror). Alfred
the Great through Harold Godwinson, chronologically forward. Æthelred the Unready
reigned twice (deposed by Swein Forkbeard in 1013, restored in 1014) -- both entries
below link to the same document.
